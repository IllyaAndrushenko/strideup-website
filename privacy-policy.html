Privacy Policy

How StrideUp collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StrideUp collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google/Gmail account information used for sign-in, such as name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Basic profile information provided through Google Sign-In.
  • Tasks, reminders, habits, progress, completion records, and experience points created in the app.
  • App usage analytics, device type, app version, crash reports, and performance data.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide authentication and account access.
  • To save and sync tasks, reminders, habits, and progress.
  • To send notifications configured by the user.
  • To improve app performance, stability, and user experience.
  • To understand feature usage through analytics.

3. Analytics

StrideUp may use analytics tools to understand app usage, detect issues, and improve performance. We do not use analytics data to serve advertisements.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Limited data may be shared only with service providers required for authentication, data storage, analytics, app operation, or legal compliance.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In for authentication.
  • Firebase / Google services for authentication, storage, analytics, and stability.
  • Analytics services for aggregated usage data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your account and related data by emailing alcs.develop@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

StrideUp is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date will be updated when changes are made.

10. Contact

For questions or requests, contact StrideUp Support: alcs.develop@gmail.com
