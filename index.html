About StrideUp

A smarter way to build habits

StrideUp is designed to help you stop forgetting the things that matter. Whether it is drinking more water, exercising regularly, or completing daily tasks — StrideUp keeps you accountable.

The app combines reminders with a simple gamification system, so building better routines feels rewarding.

  • Create and manage tasks, habits, and reminders
  • Earn experience points for every completed action
  • Track your consistency and progress over time
  • Stay motivated with a clean, focused interface
